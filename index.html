Your capital, tied to a real medicine factory — not a promised number.

Halal Income structures investment in our pharmaceutical manufacturing operation as a Shariah-compliant profit share. When the business earns, you earn a share of that real profit — typically in the 6–8% monthly range. When it doesn't, neither do we. No fixed return is ever promised, because none is guaranteed.

How it works

From your capital to the factory floor, and back as profit.

  1. 01

    You commit capital

    You register your interest and, after a conversation with our team, commit an agreed amount toward the manufacturing operation's working capital.

  2. 02

    Capital funds real production

    Funds go toward raw materials, production runs, and operating costs for our medicine manufacturing business — not toward paying returns to earlier investors.

  3. 03

    The business sells product and earns profit

    Revenue comes from selling manufactured medicine. Profit for a given month is whatever remains after real costs — it is not predetermined.

  4. 04

    Profit is shared, transparently

    A pre-agreed share of that month's actual profit (typically 6–8%, depending on performance) is distributed to investors. The remainder is retained by the operator.

The model, plainly stated

What we promise, and what we deliberately don't.

What this is

  • A profit-share in a real, operating manufacturing business
  • A return that moves up or down with actual monthly profit
  • Full visibility into how a distribution was calculated for that month

What this is not

  • Not a fixed or guaranteed monthly percentage
  • Not a multi-level referral or recruitment-based bonus scheme
  • Not a promise that your principal is risk-free

Important: all investment carries risk, including the risk of loss. Past or projected monthly figures (like the 6–8% range mentioned here) describe historical or expected performance and are not a guarantee of future results.
