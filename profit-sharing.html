The model, plainly stated

What we promise, and what we deliberately don't.

What this is

  • A profit-share in a real, operating manufacturing business
  • A return that moves up or down with actual monthly profit
  • Full visibility into how a distribution was calculated for that month

What this is not

  • Not a fixed or guaranteed monthly percentage
  • Not a multi-level referral or recruitment-based bonus scheme
  • Not a promise that your principal is risk-free

A worked example

Say the manufacturing operation generates $18,400 in verified profit in a given month, and the pre-agreed investor share for that month is 7% (within the typical 6–8% range).

ItemAmount
Total verified monthly profit$18,400
Investor share (7%)$1,288
Retained by operator (93%)$17,112

This is illustrative only. In a month with no profit, the investor share for that month is $0 — no distribution is fabricated or advanced.

Important: all investment carries risk, including the risk of loss. Past or projected monthly figures describe historical or expected performance and are not a guarantee of future results. Replace this page's terms with your client's actual, signed profit-share agreement before publishing.
